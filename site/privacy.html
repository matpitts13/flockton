Flockton

Privacy

The short version: nothing about you or your labels leaves your computer.

The apps

Flockton apps run entirely on your PC, with no accounts, analytics or crash reporting. They read only the files you give them and write only the files you save. Each app's own page lists anything it remembers between runs.

This website

This site is static pages served by Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, ads or third-party scripts. Cloudflare keeps standard server logs to run and protect the service. Download links take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.
