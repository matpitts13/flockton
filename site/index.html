Flockton

Free desktop tools for small online sellers.

Flockton is a family of small Windows apps for people who sell on TikTok Shop, Etsy and eBay. Each one does a single packing-table job well. They're free and open source, and everything runs on your own computer.

See the tools

No accounts, no subscriptions, no ads. MIT licensed.

The tools

  • Pigeonslip

    Prints shipping labels at the standard 4 × 6 in USPS size, two to a letter page. Trims the full-page label PDFs that TikTok Shop, eBay and Etsy hand you.

    Website · Download · Source

  • Robin In progress

    Thank-you stickers and package inserts. Ready-made designs or your own, in circles, squares and rectangles, for sticker sheets, cardstock or a Cricut.

  • Weaverbird Planned

    Packing slips and invoices from your TikTok Shop, Etsy or eBay order export.

  • Magpie Planned

    SKU and barcode labels for products and storage bins, on standard label sheets.

  • Egret Planned

    Batch product-photo cleanup: white backgrounds and the right crop for each marketplace, offline.

  • Nuthatch Planned

    A simple expense and receipt tracker for sellers, with a tax-ready export.

  • Kiwi Planned

    Product and cosmetic ingredient labels, with ingredients in the required order.

Your orders stay on your computer

Flockton apps don't need accounts or a connection to work. They only read the files you give them and only write the files you save.
